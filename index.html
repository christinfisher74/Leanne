Leanne’s Training Plan

Two independent workouts to complement your Monday and Thursday coaching sessions. Train with purpose, track your progress, and adjust to your recovery.

Workout 1 · Back strength Workout 2 · Lower body + core Dumbbells + landmine
How to use this plan: These sessions are supplemental. Schedule them on other days when recovery allows; you do not need to force all four weekly sessions if work fatigue is high. Aim for about 2–3 good reps left at the end of each set.

Back Strength

Lats · Mid-back · Rear delts · Biceps

Approx. 35–45 min
1

Landmine Single-Arm Bent-Over Row

3 sets × 8–12 reps per side · Rest 60–90 sec

Brace as needed, keep your torso stable, and avoid twisting to finish each rep.

2

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

3 sets × 10–12 reps per side · Rest 60–90 sec

Support your free hand on a stable surface; pull your elbow toward your hip without rotating your torso.

3

Landmine Bent-Over Row — Two-Hand Grip

3 sets × 10–12 reps · Rest 60–90 sec

Use a comfortable grip and steady hinge; avoid jerking or using excessive torso momentum.

4

Bent-Over Dumbbell Rear-Delt Raise

2–3 sets × 12–15 reps · Rest 45–60 sec

Choose a light load, keep elbows soft, and use a comfortable range. Skip if it brings on pec or shoulder symptoms.

5

Dumbbell Hammer Curl

3 sets × 10–12 reps · Rest 45–60 sec

Keep elbows close to your sides and lower the weights under control.

Lower Body + Core

Quads · Glutes · Hamstrings · Calves · Trunk

Approx. 35–45 min
1

Goblet Box Squat

3 sets × 10–12 reps · Rest 60–90 sec

Use a consistent depth and keep pressure even through both feet.

2

Dumbbell Lateral Lunge

3 sets × 8 reps per side · Rest 60–75 sec

Use a range you can control and sit into the working hip without forcing depth.

3

Glute Bridge Walkouts

3 sets × 6–10 walkouts · Rest 60 sec

Start in a bridge, take small heel steps away and back, and keep hips controlled. Shorten the walkout if your hips drop.

4

Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

2 sets × 8–10 reps · Rest 75–90 sec

Use a moderate load and crisp hip hinge. Keep volume modest alongside your coached hinge work.

5

Side-Lying Hip Abduction

3 sets × 12–15 reps per side · Rest 45–60 sec

Keep hips stacked and lift without rolling the pelvis backward.

6

Standing Calf Raise

3 sets × 12–15 reps · Rest 45–60 sec

Use stable support if needed and pause briefly at the top.

7

Dead Bug

3 sets × 8 reps per side · Rest 45 sec

Keep ribs down and maintain control through the trunk as the opposite arm and leg move.

Training guidance

  • Start around effort level 7/10, leaving about 2–3 good reps in reserve.
  • Progress reps first; increase load only when all reps are controlled.
  • No overhead presses or chest presses are included.
  • Stop any movement that causes pec or shoulder pain, pinching, or worsening tightness; contact Christin to adjust it.
  • These sessions complement your coached workouts. If work fatigue is high, reduce a set or skip an accessory rather than forcing the full session.
